Limitations

Records, not relationships

The tool knows where a business is, what it does and what Companies House holds on it. It can't see the owner, their budget, whether they already back another club, or who at Dumbarton knows them. A phone call will tell you more than any score here.

Registered companies only

Sole traders and partnerships aren't on Companies House, so many chippies, trades and shops show as Unclassified, score lower on trading record than they deserve, and have no employee or revenue figures. A registered office can also be the accountant's address, not the shop.

Estimates are averages

An estimated revenue is the typical turnover for that industry and size, the same for every similar firm. It says roughly what scale of business this is, not what this business earns.

AI readings are judgments

The website readings are made by an AI model from the business's own homepage and haven't been checked by a person. A thin or missing website holds a reading at the middle so it can't help or hurt.

A set of choices, not a measurement

The weights put local ties ahead of size, and the sector rates are hand-set from how lower-league clubs are usually sponsored. Both are judgments the club can change. Treat close scores as a tie.
